# Manufacturer Hint File — North Eden Timber

## Scope and grounding

- Use only facts explicitly present in the supplied North Eden Timber documents.
- The eight two-page species reference guides are supporting technical references. They describe timber species, not separate product systems.
- Do not invent SKUs or product codes; the current documents generally identify products by profile name and dimensions.
- Preserve source spellings but normalize `Silver top Ash` to `Silvertop Ash`, `Brownbarrel` to `Brown Barrel`, and `Red Gum` to `Forest Red Gum` where the source makes the identity clear.

## Product systems

Create these systems when supported by the source:

- North Eden Timber Allure Cladding
- North Eden Timber Aura Cladding
- North Eden Timber Contour Cladding
- North Eden Timber Rusticated Cladding
- North Eden Timber Traditional Weatherboard Cladding
- North Eden Timber Log Cabin Cladding
- North Eden Timber Habitat Cladding
- North Eden Timber Domain Cladding
- North Eden Timber Watershield Decking

Do not create systems for generic headings such as `Australian Hardwood Cladding`, `Classic Overlap Cladding`, `Speciality Profiles`, installation instructions, maintenance, ordering, galleries, or individual timber species.

## Profiles and variants

- Treat each documented size as a system profile/variant, retaining its exact dimensions and effective cover where present.
- For Aura, each width can have documented thickness variants 19mm, 26mm and 32mm; do not generate combinations unless the dimensions are explicitly stated in the source.
- Timber species (Ironbark, Spotted Gum, Blackbutt, Forest Red Gum, Silvertop Ash, Brown Barrel, White Mahogany and White Cypress) are material/finish availability variants, not standalone systems.
- `available on request` must remain a note and must not be converted to stocked availability.

## Ratings

- Apply BAL ratings only to the timber species explicitly listed for that rating. Do not assign a single species rating to every profile or system.
- Treat durability, Janka rating, density and termite resistance as timber-species properties, not profile dimensions.

## Components

- Extract only named installation accessories or starter boards explicitly offered as separate items.
- Do not turn battens, membranes, nails or generic installation materials into North Eden Timber components unless the source clearly sells or names them as products.
